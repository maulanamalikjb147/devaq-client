Write a useful buying brief

Give each product its own line. Name the grade and purpose, identify specifications that must be met and include a sample reference if one has already been approved. Separate trial quantities from the intended commercial order.

Product
Grade code, material, size, target properties and intended use.
Packing
Net fill, inner pack count, outer pack and label requirements.
Quantity
Requested sample and order size, both with units.
Delivery
Receiving city or port, target arrival and any timing flexibility.
Supplier response
Unit price, currency, quote validity, preparation time and scope of freight.

A worked inquiry for tea-cut hibiscus

We are evaluating HB02 tea-cut hibiscus for portioned tea bags. Please quote 200 kg in ten 20 kg lined sacks, with delivery to Jakarta itemized separately. For the trial, can you supply a 200 g sample and a lot-specific size distribution for the 1 to 3 mm cut?

This fictional buyer has given the supplier a grade, sample size, pack count and destination. The reply can now identify the lot, confirm the specification and show which costs change if the destination or quantity changes.

Turn the MOQ into a pack count

Minimum order quantity is the smallest commercial order in the example listing. Divide the proposed net order weight by the net weight in one outer pack. Check whether the supplier accepts part packs or requires complete cartons.

CH02 coconut shell cubes
500 kg ÷ 10 kg per carton = 50 cartons. Each carton contains ten 1 kg inner boxes.
HB01 whole calyces
100 kg ÷ 10 kg per carton = 10 cartons.
HB03 hibiscus powder
50 kg ÷ 5 kg per pouch = 10 pouches.

Samples have their own quantities. Request sample cost and dispatch terms separately so the trial does not get mistaken for a commercial order.

Specify a private-label pack

For a retail inquiry, send the artwork size, finished pack dimensions and the text that must appear on the inner and outer packs. Ask the supplier to quote printing separately and to provide a proof before production.

For example, an order of 500 kg of CH02 in 1 kg retail boxes requires 500 printed boxes and 50 outer cartons. A printer may require a larger run. Ask how many unused boxes would remain, who would hold them and how a subsequent artwork change would be handled.

Buyer questions

Can I combine two grades to meet an MOQ?

The example MOQs apply to individual grades. List the quantity for each grade and ask the supplier to confirm whether a mixed order is possible.

What does preparation time include?

In the catalog examples, it is the period between approval of the sample and packing details and readiness for dispatch. Request a separate estimate for transit and delivery.

What if I need a different cut, size or pack?

Include the closest catalog code, the required change and the quantity. Ask whether the change affects the sample, minimum order or preparation time.

What should I keep after approving a sample?

Save a labeled reference sample, its lot code, dated photographs, the agreed specification and the quotation that refers to it. Give the receiving team access to the same record.
