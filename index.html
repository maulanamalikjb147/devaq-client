Operations

From field to freight.

Use these checkpoints to follow a proposed lot from its source to the receiving warehouse.

Source the material

For charcoal, request the feedstock and its origin. For roselle, record the growing region, harvest period and drying method. Keep the supplier’s lot reference with the sample.

Check the grade

Measure charcoal pieces against the agreed size range and record the fines in the sample. For hibiscus, compare the cut and color with the approved sample and request the lot’s moisture result.

Approve the pack

A charcoal distributor may need ten retail boxes inside one carton. A tea blender may prefer a single lined sack. Agree the net fill, closure, lot marking and outer-pack count before dispatch.

Record the handover

Match carton counts and gross weights to the packing list. Request loading photos and the transport reference so the receiving team can identify the consignment on arrival.

Buyer FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Essential guidance on requesting sample parcels, custom OEM packaging runs, international compliance certifications, and ocean shipping terms.

How can our team request physical product samples before ordering?

We supply lab-certified sample parcels (typically 500g–1kg for charcoal grades and 100g–250g for roselle botanicals) drawn directly from current production batches. Each sample includes a lot Certificate of Analysis (COA). Buyers provide their DHL/FedEx courier account or we invoice international express air freight at cost.

Can you manufacture private-label boxes and custom OEM packaging?

Yes. We offer turnkey private labeling. You provide your vector artwork, barcodes, and die-lines, and we handle carton printing, food-grade inner film sealing, and customized master shipping cases. Custom box printing typically requires an MOQ starting from 500 to 1,000 units.

What international export documents and certifications do you supply?

Every export shipment includes a Certificate of Analysis (COA), Commercial Invoice, Detailed Packing List, and Bill of Lading (B/L). For charcoal, we issue material safety data sheets (MSDS) and self-heating test reports (SHT). For hibiscus, we supply official Phytosanitary Certificates and Certificate of Origin (COO Form D / Form E / Form AK / Form A).

What are your standard minimum order quantities (MOQ) and production lead times?

Trial commercial orders begin at 500 kg to 1,000 kg for palletized LCL shipments, while full container load (FCL) orders range between 18–26 metric tons depending on the product bulk density. Standard preparation and packing time is 14 to 21 business days upon contract confirmation and deposit.

Which Incoterms and international payment structures do you support?

We routinely export under Incoterms 2020: FOB (Tanjung Priok, Jakarta or Tanjung Perak, Surabaya), CIF, CFR, or EXW. Standard payment terms are Telegraphic Transfer (T/T with 30–50% advance deposit and balance against Bill of Lading copy) or Confirmed Irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C at sight) for container volumes.

Can we consolidate multiple product grades within a single shipping container?

Yes. Multi-grade consolidation is supported (e.g. combining coconut shell cubes with hardwood lump, or combining whole roselle calyces with tea-cut cuts). We arrange dedicated pallet zoning and container weight distribution plans to ensure transit stability and seamless customs clearance.
